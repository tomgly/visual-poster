# Playful Naive Editorial

**ID:** `playful-naive-editorial`

## Core Direction

Reinterpret the subject as a playful, naive, retro editorial illustration combining modernist composition, Bauhaus-inspired graphic rhythm, children's picture-book sensibility, naive art, and fashion sketching.

The original subject must remain instantly recognizable.

## Transformation

Do not mechanically reproduce photographic detail.

Instead:

- summarize forms;
- exaggerate selected proportions;
- simplify silhouettes;
- enlarge expressive features when useful;
- translate details into symbolic characteristics;
- introduce humorous visual metaphors;
- retain recognizable identity, pose, and narrative relationships.

Contours may contain slight hesitation, asymmetry, and handmade error.

## Composition

Use large areas of breathing room.

Support the subject with restrained elements such as:

- pale color fields;
- loose hatching;
- simple geometry;
- isolated graphic symbols.

The composition should feel editorial and intentionally imperfect.

## Color

Default:

```text
COLOR_MODE = source-derived
```

Use a soft limited palette with:

- high lightness;
- low-to-medium saturation;
- analogous harmony;
- small complementary accents.

## Materials

Blend:

- watercolor;
- gouache;
- colored pencil;
- pastel;
- dry brush.

Preserve:

- paper grain;
- translucency;
- broken brush marks;
- rough edges;
- small stains;
- imperfect registration;
- vintage-print offset.

## Typography

Default:

```text
TEXT_MODE = editorial
```

Possible content:

- short English keywords;
- titles;
- numbers;
- short phrases.

Primary type may use naive retro hand lettering.

Secondary type may use restrained serif or sans-serif forms.

Allow subtle:

- misalignment;
- tilt;
- uneven scaling;
- overlapping color;
- print misregistration.

Typography should participate in the illustration rather than appear as an added label.

## Mood

```text
relaxed
playful
retro
gentle
romantic
clever
refined
humorous
slightly awkward
confidently eccentric
```

## Avoid

- dense realistic scenery;
- photorealistic lighting;
- polished vector graphics;
- 3D rendering;
- excessive retouching;
- generic cartoon styling;
- commercial template aesthetics.
