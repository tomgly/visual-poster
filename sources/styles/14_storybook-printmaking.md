# Storybook Printmaking

**ID:** `storybook-printmaking`

## Core Direction

Transform the subject into a handmade storybook-print composition combining bold structural fields, simplified silhouettes, narrative color signals, and tactile printmaking texture.

The subject should remain highly recognizable despite low detail.

## Transformation

Compress the subject into one or two clear graphic masses.

Use:

- heavy simplified silhouettes;
- slightly exaggerated proportions;
- a small number of defining edges.

Preserve the subject's:

- identity;
- action;
- function;
- emotion;
- narrative role.

Build the surrounding space as a structural narrative field rather than a realistic background.

It may behave like:

- a container;
- path;
- frame;
- density layer;
- enclosing shell.

Use cropping, concealment, partial exposure, and spatial pressure to create story.

## Composition

Use flat storybook perspective and strong page-like hierarchy.

Possible devices include:

- compressed overhead view;
- lateral movement;
- frontal framing;
- directional paths;
- scale jumps;
- edge cropping.

## Color

Use a three-level hierarchy:

1. large dark structural field;
2. light field or negative space;
3. small high-saturation narrative signals.

Bright accents should identify important elements such as:

- face;
- gesture;
- path;
- functional object;
- environmental cue;
- narrative focus.

Do not distribute bright colors evenly.

## Materials

Use flat printed color with handmade texture.

Preserve:

- dry brush;
- grain;
- broken pigment;
- paper texture;
- spray-like dots;
- slight print misregistration.

The surface may evoke screen printing combined with pastel or crayon-like printmaking.

## Typography

Default:

```text
TEXT_MODE = micro
```

When used, typography should behave like:

- a small sign;
- object label;
- edge annotation.

Keep it sparse, rounded, slightly uneven, and naturally handmade.

## Mood

```text
narrative
warm
bold
handmade
childlike without being childish
graphic
tactile
```

## Avoid

- realistic lighting;
- complex volumetric rendering;
- smooth vector edges;
- 3D rendering;
- plastic surfaces;
- excessive detail;
- generic commercial cartoon styling.
