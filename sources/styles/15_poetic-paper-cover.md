# Poetic Paper Cover

**ID:** `poetic-paper-cover`

## Core Direction

Create an extremely reduced hand-drawn cover illustration built around a very small recognizable subject and a very large field of quiet paper.

The result should evoke an art-book cover, independent publication, or refined picture-book design.

## Transformation

Remove complex detail aggressively.

Keep only the most essential visual characteristics.

Represent the subject using:

- thin slightly unstable hand-drawn lines;
- a small number of clear flat acrylic-like color shapes.

The original subject must remain immediately recognizable.

## Composition

Default subject scale:

```text
approximately 10–20% of the canvas
```

Surround the subject with extensive negative space.

Use rough white or pale paper as the primary field.

Environmental context should be suggested only through a very small number of:

- lines;
- flat shapes;
- minimal visual cues.

## Color

Default:

```text
maximum 4 primary colors
```

Derive the palette from the source when appropriate.

Keep color blocks:

- clear;
- complete;
- restrained;
- flat.

## Materials

Preserve:

- paper grain;
- handmade paint application;
- slightly irregular edges.

The line establishes structure.

The flat color establishes the subject.

## Typography

Default:

```text
TEXT_MODE = minimal
```

Possible content:

- title;
- theme word;
- subject name;
- place;
- year;
- number;
- short sentence.

Typography should be sparse and naturally integrated into the blank space.

## Mood

```text
quiet
light
poetic
gentle
refined
playful
highly distilled
```

## Avoid

- dense illustration;
- colored-pencil rendering;
- crayon rendering;
- watercolor bleeding;
- heavy oil-paint texture;
- smooth digital illustration;
- complex realism;
- 3D surfaces;
- commercial cartoon styling;
- template aesthetics.
