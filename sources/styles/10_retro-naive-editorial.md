# Retro Naive Editorial

**ID:** `retro-naive-editorial`

## Core Direction

Transform the subject into a light, restrained, retro hand-drawn editorial illustration with the warmth of vintage fashion picture books, travel illustration, art publishing, and sophisticated editorial design.

The source must remain immediately recognizable.

## Transformation

Simplify the subject into concise shapes and recognizable silhouettes.

Avoid literal tracing and excessive detail.

Use:

- gentle proportion simplification;
- delicate hand-drawn contours;
- subtle handmade irregularity;
- restrained visual symbols;
- simplified environmental cues.

Keep the result intelligent and editorial rather than overtly cartoonish.

## Composition

Use generous off-white or pale negative space.

Keep the composition airy, balanced, and relaxed.

Small hand-drawn lines, geometric symbols, or abstract marks may support the subject without filling the page.

## Color

Default:

```text
COLOR_MODE = source-derived
```

Extract a limited palette from the source.

Favor:

- high lightness;
- low-to-medium saturation;
- analogous color harmony;
- small complementary accents.

## Materials

Combine selected qualities of:

- watercolor;
- gouache;
- colored pencil;
- oil pastel;
- dry brush.

Preserve:

- paper grain;
- exposed paper;
- broken brushwork;
- rough edges;
- subtle misregistration;
- slight pigment irregularity.

## Typography

Default:

```text
TEXT_MODE = minimal
```

Use typography sparingly.

Suitable elements include:

- short handwritten titles;
- years;
- numbers;
- small editorial notes.

Typography should feel naturally integrated into the illustration.

## Mood

```text
gentle
nostalgic
relaxed
stylish
intelligent
slightly playful
quietly eccentric
```

## Avoid

- literal tracing;
- dense detail;
- glossy digital illustration;
- smooth vector perfection;
- photorealistic illustration;
- 3D rendering;
- plastic surfaces;
- exaggerated cartoon styling;
- cheap commercial aesthetics;
- template-driven layouts.
