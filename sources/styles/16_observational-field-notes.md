# Observational Field Notes

**ID:** `observational-field-notes`

## Core Direction

Translate the subject into a relaxed hand-drawn observation page combining small illustrated fragments, close-up details, handwritten notes, and generous white space.

The result should feel like an intimate visual notebook rather than a formal infographic.

## Transformation

Break the subject into several small recognizable components such as:

- object studies;
- detail fragments;
- close-ups;
- cross-sections;
- repeated small items;
- material details.

Distribute them loosely across the upper-middle and center of the page.

Do not use a strict table or rigid grid.

Create an informal horizontal reading rhythm through spacing and handwritten notes.

## Illustration

Small illustrations should preserve the subject's:

- material;
- characteristic color;
- recognizable form;
- surface detail.

Use:

- rounded hand-drawn contours;
- slight perspective;
- handmade coloring;
- localized grain;
- fragments;
- texture;
- cutaway details when relevant.

Avoid converting everything into standardized icons.

## Typography

Default:

```text
TEXT_MODE = annotated
```

Typography is a major part of the composition.

Use thin black handwritten text with subtle naive irregularity.

Possible text includes:

- short words;
- short phrases;
- sound effects;
- observations;
- labels;
- sensory notes.

Place text beside, near, or between illustrations.

Allow:

- relaxed spacing;
- gently uneven baselines;
- floating annotations.

A small signature-like line, date, number, title, or concluding phrase may appear near the bottom.

## Color

Use white paper as the dominant field.

Use black thin writing for information.

Subject colors should feel:

- warm;
- clean;
- slightly bright.

Use stronger accents only for:

- focal points;
- texture;
- rhythm;
- small shadows;
- key identifying details.

## Materials

Preserve subtle scanned-paper softness, pigment absorption, uneven edges, and handmade surface variation.

## Mood

```text
fresh
gentle
observational
intimate
lively
readable
casual
human
```

## Avoid

- rigid infographic grids;
- standardized icon systems;
- heavy shadows;
- decorative frames;
- slick contemporary UI aesthetics;
- cartoon simplification;
- cheap template styling;
- over-designed layouts.
