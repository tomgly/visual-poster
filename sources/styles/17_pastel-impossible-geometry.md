# Pastel Impossible Geometry

**ID:** `pastel-impossible-geometry`

## Core Direction

Translate the subject's identity, emotional relationships, and narrative structure into a clean minimal 3D world of poetic impossible geometry.

The source should remain conceptually and visually recognizable through spatial metaphor rather than literal reconstruction.

## Transformation

Translate essential subject characteristics into architectural elements such as:

- platforms;
- stairs;
- gates;
- arches;
- passages;
- floating structures;
- impossible connections.

Use spatial relationships such as:

- height differences;
- pathways;
- connection;
- obstruction;
- isolation;
- repetition;
- separation.

The geometry should express the emotional and narrative relationship found in the source.

## Perspective

Use orthographic or isometric-style projection.

Keep forms:

- simple;
- precise;
- poetic;
- quiet;
- spatially legible.

## Color

Default:

```text
COLOR_MODE = style-default
```

Do not directly copy the complete source palette.

Choose 2–4 primary colors from a luminous pastel system such as:

- cream white;
- pale sandstone;
- mist pink;
- coral;
- soft violet;
- sky blue;
- lake blue;
- mint green.

Use adjacent hues and warm/cool contrast to create harmony.

Allow one small high-purity accent such as:

- orange-red;
- rose;
- teal;
- bright yellow.

Use large clean color fields and clear value separation.

## Materials and Lighting

Surfaces should be:

- extremely clean;
- smooth;
- matte;
- solid-colored.

Use soft even lighting and subtle environmental shadows.

The image should feel clear, luminous, and airy.

## Typography

Default:

```text
TEXT_MODE = micro
```

Use only small editorial annotations.

Possible content:

- one short poetic sentence;
- a subtle translation;
- a restrained thematic phrase.

Keep typography small, thin, geometric, and widely spaced.

Place it quietly near:

- an edge;
- a corner;
- architectural negative space;
- the lower margin.

Do not use dates, volume numbers, or oversized titles by default.

## Mood

```text
bright
pure
dreamlike
quiet
intelligent
poetic
precise
restrained
```

## Avoid

- paper texture;
- grain;
- noise;
- film texture;
- vintage printing effects;
- dirt;
- frosted surfaces;
- complex PBR materials;
- strong reflections;
- muddy colors;
- heavy brown;
- large black areas;
- low-value color schemes.
