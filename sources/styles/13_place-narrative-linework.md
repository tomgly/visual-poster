# Place Narrative Linework

**ID:** `place-narrative-linework`

## Core Direction

Distill the most recognizable identity of a place, scene, building, subject, or theme into refined minimal linework with architectural clarity and editorial restraint.

This preset is especially suitable for places, streets, neighborhoods, architecture, travel subjects, and spatial narratives.

## Place Interpretation

When the input refers to a real place, prioritize recognizable characteristics such as:

- street structure;
- architectural facades;
- skyline;
- storefront rhythm;
- signage relationships;
- transportation elements;
- windows and display fronts;
- human activity;
- spatial hierarchy.

Do not replace location-specific identity with generic scenery.

## Transformation

Extract the most important:

- silhouette;
- structural relationship;
- perspective;
- spatial rhythm;
- recognizable detail;
- narrative relationship.

Reconstruct them using:

- thin precise lines;
- clear geometric perspective;
- controlled abstraction;
- orderly detail rhythm;
- generous negative space.

Avoid literal mechanical tracing.

## Composition

Use a clean modern poster structure.

Small amounts of the following may support the composition:

- horizontal lines;
- vertical construction lines;
- geometric guides;
- rhythmic spacing.

The result should feel like a designed extraction of the subject rather than a technical blueprint.

## Color

Use a restrained theme-specific palette rather than a fixed universal color scheme.

Consider:

- light;
- climate;
- material;
- atmosphere;
- history;
- emotional temperature.

Favor monochrome or a very limited palette.

Create depth through:

- line density;
- value hierarchy;
- sparse accents;
- negative space.

## Typography

Default:

```text
TEXT_MODE = editorial
```

Suitable content includes:

- title;
- original place name;
- local language;
- region;
- address;
- coordinates;
- year;
- number;
- one short contextual line.

Typography should feel integrated into a collectible travel poster, architectural publication, or cultural identity system.

## Mood

```text
precise
quiet
modern
refined
observational
architectural
collectible
```

## Avoid

- postcard clichés;
- cheap tourism aesthetics;
- generic landmark exaggeration;
- dense collage;
- fake or random signage;
- photorealistic illustration;
- heavy 3D material rendering;
- excessive color.
